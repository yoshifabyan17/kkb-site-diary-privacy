Privacy notice

Updated 25 October 2026

Sample-only test. This notice covers the current development test. It does not open the service to live site records or wider staff use.

Who is responsible

KKB Group Ltd decides how this service uses personal information. In this notice, “we” means KKB Group Ltd.

For questions or requests about your information, contact Yash Bhanot at yash.bhanot@kkbgroup.co.uk.

What the test does

We are testing a service that receives WhatsApp site-diary updates and saves them in a database and private file store. We check that messages and files arrive, remain linked to their source and do not create duplicate records.

The current capture test is limited to one approved test sender. Publishing this notice does not add any other sender or project. The service is still being checked and is not a reliable site reporting channel.

Only approved test projects may use this version. Users take part through the test project they work on, rather than joining a separate test group. Working on a test project does not by itself opt you in.

By sending a message to the WhatsApp number for your approved test project, after you have been given this notice, you opt in to taking part in that project's test. Taking part is optional. You can stop taking part by no longer sending test messages and contacting Yash Bhanot. This does not automatically delete information already held; your information rights are explained below.

For the current capture test, send made-up site information. Do not send real site records, passwords, identity documents, health information or unnecessary information about other people. Do not use this number for emergencies or safety reports. This test does not replace your normal project reporting process.

Information we receive

We receive your WhatsApp phone number, profile name where supplied, message text, captions and any photos, video or voice notes you send. Files may include information about people who appear or speak in them.

We also receive message identifiers, sender and receiving-account identifiers, message times and delivery information from Meta. Our service can save the original incoming notification, file details and records of capture errors. Some information may come from another person working on the test project who includes it in a message.

We receive information sent to this service. We do not gain access to the rest of your WhatsApp conversations or phone contacts.

Why we use it

We use the information to test receipt and private storage, find faults, protect the service and respond to privacy requests. AI processing is not enabled in this test. The service does not make automated decisions about employment, pay or access to work.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: reliable site recording to support safe working and good practice in KKB's construction delivery. Opting in means choosing to take part in the project test; it does not change that lawful basis. Sending a message is not blanket consent for other uses or consent on behalf of other people mentioned in it.

Who handles it and where

WhatsApp and Meta handle message delivery. Supabase provides temporary database and private file storage for this test. Supabase Pte. Ltd., in Singapore, is its primary data processor. The selected database and file-storage region is London.

The test database currently has one account owner: the KKB build lead. It was set up through a personal sign-in account. The database and files are not public. KKB's staff-access policy is limited to management and the data team working from the UK. This temporary test does not have a technical control that blocks every login from outside the UK, and wider staff access has not been enabled.

Some provider support, service logs, account handling and message delivery can take place outside the UK. Supabase's data processing terms include standard contractual clauses and UK transfer provisions. Its data-location guide explains overseas processing. WhatsApp publishes UK transfer terms. Contact Yash for information about the safeguards relevant to this service. These provider terms do not mean that all processing stays in the UK.

WhatsApp also handles information under its own privacy policy. This notice describes our use of information for the KKB service.

How long we keep it

We are exploring automatic deletion of test messages, files and related personal information after 12 months from receipt. This is a likely retention point, not a fixed deletion deadline. Automatic deletion is not enabled.

KKB will run a retention audit at the end of the year. The audit will assess whether information is still needed for site recording, audit purposes, a legal requirement or a legal claim. Information may be kept longer where that need remains. The reason for continued retention and the next review date must be recorded.

The year-end review will also cover service records, privacy-request records and any copies KKB controls. Privacy-request records are kept only as needed to handle the request and show the response. Provider service logs have their own retention periods and may expire earlier. The current free test plan has no project-backup feature enabled. If backup arrangements change, we will include those copies in the review.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restricted use or a copy in a portable format.

You can object to use based on legitimate interests. Contact us using the details above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

How to request deletion

Email Yash Bhanot and say which information you want deleted. You can use the subject “KKB Site Diary data deletion”. Include your WhatsApp number and approximate message dates if these help us find the records. These words and details are not compulsory. You can also make a request verbally to KKB.

Do not send passwords or identity documents with your first request. We may ask for limited information to confirm your identity. We will normally respond within one calendar month. If a lawful extension applies, we will explain the reason and revised timescale.

Where deletion is required, it covers the related messages, original notifications, files and other copies we control. If we must retain some information, we will explain why. Deleting a message in WhatsApp does not by itself delete a copy already saved by our service. We cannot delete copies held independently by other people or providers.

Read the separate deletion instructions.

When you visit these pages

GitHub Pages hosts this notice and the deletion instructions. These pages have no forms, analytics code or advertising. They do not display messages or give access to the database.

GitHub receives technical request information, including your IP address, when it serves the pages. It may process this outside the UK under its own privacy statement, which explains its retention and transfer safeguards. GitHub does not host the test message database or file store.

Changes to this service

We will review this notice before adding live site users, AI processing, new data sources or a different hosting arrangement. We will explain relevant changes before the new use starts.
