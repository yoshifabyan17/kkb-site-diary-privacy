Draft updated 23 October 2026

Draft for KKB review — not approved for publication. These instructions cover the KKB Site Diary WhatsApp test. Read the draft privacy notice for the test limits and open review points.

Contact KKB

Email Yash Bhanot at yash.bhanot@kkbgroup.co.uk and say which information you want deleted.

You can use the subject “KKB Site Diary data deletion”. Include your WhatsApp number and approximate message dates if these help us find the records. These words and details are not compulsory. You can also make a request verbally to KKB.

Do not send passwords or identity documents with your first request.

What happens next

We may ask for limited information to confirm your identity. We will normally respond within one calendar month. If a lawful extension applies, we will explain the reason and revised timescale.

Where deletion is required, it covers the related messages, original notifications, files and other copies we control. If we must retain some information, we will explain why.

What deleting a WhatsApp message does

Deleting a message in WhatsApp does not by itself delete a copy already saved by our service. We cannot delete copies held independently by other people or providers.

Automatic deletion is not enabled. KKB will review retention at the end of the year. See how long we keep information.

If you have a concern

Contact Yash using the details above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
