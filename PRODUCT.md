## Stack

## Users

## Product Purpose

Provide the privacy notice and deletion instructions only. This is not the automation service or a database interface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

No database, login, forms, tracking code, external fonts or JavaScript. Keep the automation repository and captured data private. The notice remains a draft until its open publication checks are resolved. Do not enable public hosting or enter draft URLs in Meta. Do not enable automatic deletion, AI processing or live site collection.

## Brand Commitments

Simple text-only page, as requested. Use KKB Group Ltd and privacy contact Yash Bhanot. Preserve project-led participation and the legitimate-interests basis in the source notice.

## Evidence on Hand

The controlled draft notice, updated 23 October 2026, and its separate publication review are held in the private automation project. SharePoint remains the controlled document home. Provider terms do not prove KKB-specific access or contract arrangements.

## Product Principles

- Keep the notice readable on a phone.
- Keep privacy requests easy to find.
- Preserve the draft status and open checks.
- Keep project data separate from these pages.
